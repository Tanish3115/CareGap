# Structured records and care coordination

Implemented locally on 1 November 2026. No GitHub push or external deployment is authorized.

Nine clinical consent groups now limit these records, summaries, source histories and imports/exports. Patients may narrow requested groups. A unified current-record timeline combines the previously separate workspaces with type/date/search filters, provenance, source downloads and history. Clinical sharing still includes explicitly disclosed all-document access; it does not redact original files. See [CONSENT_SCOPES.md](CONSENT_SCOPES.md).

The dashboard now includes Clinical records & care events and Care coordination & source overview. Patients can record source-linked diagnoses, allergies/reactions, literal lab results, appointments, referrals and recommended tests. Care outcomes distinguish pending/completed/missed/cancelled/unknown, require reports for terminal states and preserve earlier versions. Rescheduling requests retain their requested date/reason; only consented clinicians can approve/decline, with a review note. Date-only care records do not reserve a real appointment slot.

Verified clinicians with explicit clinical write consent can record separate medication orders, dispensing and clinician medication-use accounts, and staff-reviewed interventions. These are manually documented events, not automated prescribing or pharmacy transactions. Medication names/doses stay literal; missing fields remain null. Optional terminology code/system/evidence must be recorded together; no brand equivalence or approved mapping is invented.

Clinical identities, ownership, authors, source associations and revision relationships use foreign keys. A shared revision table stores strict discriminated Pydantic payloads for ten resource kinds. This deliberately shares version/provenance plumbing while avoiding unvalidated free-form JSON: unknown fields, inconsistent medication dates, future account dates, incomplete terminal outcomes, kind changes and patient-authored clinician-only events are rejected. Clinical fields are not yet mapped to a complete production terminology model. All updates use expected versions; source uploads remain unchanged.

The care worklist selects unresolved structured care events due within seven days, labels overdue recorded plans and suggests transparent staff-review actions based on explicitly reported barriers. It does not predict treatment effects or silently treat unknown outcomes as missed care. Consultation-linked follow-ups retain their separate existing list.

Notification preferences are patient-owned and default off. The in-app/email/SMS/WhatsApp channel abstraction currently records local mock deliveries only, with generic English/Hindi templates and idempotency by record/version/channel/day. No contacts, clinical messages or external provider calls are sent. Opt-out and record changes serialize against delivery creation. History flags reminders whose linked record later changed. Queueing requires a reviewed current care version and write authorization.

Medication comparison indicators identify exact repeated names, differing literal dose text, explicitly overlapping recorded periods and incomplete historical prescriptions. They never infer current use, therapeutic equivalence, a safe dose or real interactions. The synthetic A/B interaction and allergy-token fixtures are displayed separately as software demonstrations. `MedicationKnowledgeProvider` defines the evidence/version contract for a future validated provider; no real clinical knowledge provider is configured.

The source overview extracts authorized structured records, consultations, follow-ups, historical prescriptions and patient-use accounts into independently paged source-linked facts. It emits no generated clinical conclusions or AI interpretations. Empty pages mean unrecorded/outside this page, not confirmed absence. This is an extractive overview, not a deployed LLM summarizer.

Organization metadata and memberships never grant clinical access. Doctors may request consent in personal capacity or for a verified organization they belong to. Organization-bound grants are rechecked on every read. Membership/organization deactivation revokes those grants; reactivation does not resurrect them. Personal grants remain distinct. Operational staff and platform administrators have no automatic clinical access.

Patient exports include paged account/clinical histories and source relationships without passwords/session tokens/infrastructure secrets. Original files and imported JSON have authenticated retrieval routes. Privacy requests support administrative review; closing one does not execute erasure. Full account/source/object/backup erasure requires an approved retention policy and separately reviewed execution tooling. Patient-owned consultation deletion continues to work as before.

## FHIR R4 subset

Exports are collection Bundles of structured records with patient references and provenance labels, not executable transactions. Conditions, recorded allergies, simple observations, medication requests/dispenses/statements and care plans have distinct mappings. Date-only appointment plans use Tasks rather than invented booked times. The medication distinction follows the [HL7 R4 MedicationStatement boundaries](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/medicationstatement.html); collection behavior follows the [HL7 Bundle definition](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/bundle.html).

Import is deliberately narrower: one explicitly matching internal Patient UUID plus Condition, AllergyIntolerance and simple valueString Observation resources. Unsupported resource types, external/foreign subjects, transactions, quantities/coded interpretations, malformed/oversized inputs and duplicate exact bundles are rejected. Preview and source confirmation precede import. Original JSON/checksum and derivation links remain after corrections; imported assertions do not gain independent clinical verification. Unknown allergy clinical status uses an explicit absent-reason extension to preserve the [R4 status constraint](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/allergyintolerance.html), rather than inventing active status.

This adapter has tested application mappings, not full FHIR conformance certification. General import, practitioner/organization terminology profiles, document/binary exchange, full medication history export and ABDM integration remain further work. ABDM requires actual authorization, identity/consent flows, sandbox validation and conformance; see the [government sandbox guidance](https://www.india.gov.in/category/health-wellness/subcategory/health-care-promotion-products/details/ayushman-bharat-digital-mission-abdm-sandbox-guidelines). No live ABDM access is claimed.
